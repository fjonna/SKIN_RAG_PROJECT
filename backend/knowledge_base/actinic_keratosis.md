# Actinic Keratosis

## Overview
Actinic keratosis is a rough, scaly growth on chronically sun-exposed skin that should be professionally assessed.

## Skin Symptoms
Rough scaly patch, dry crusted spot, sandpaper texture, pink, red, or brown patch, and tenderness.

## Visual Appearance
Often a persistent rough spot on the face, ears, scalp, hands, or neck.

## Possible Causes
Long-term ultraviolet exposure from sunlight or indoor tanning.

## Risk Groups
People with substantial sun exposure, lighter skin, older age, or reduced immune function.

## Risk Level
Moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Use sun protection and arrange a skin examination.

## Warning Signs
Promptly assess a new, changing, painful, bleeding, thickening, or nonhealing spot.
