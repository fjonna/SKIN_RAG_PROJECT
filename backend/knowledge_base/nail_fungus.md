# Nail Fungus

## Overview
Nail fungus is a fungal infection that can make nails thick, discolored, brittle, or distorted.

## Skin Symptoms
Thickened nail, yellow nail, brown nail, brittle nail, crumbly nail, distorted nail, nail debris, and lifting nail.

## Visual Appearance
One or more nails may become yellow-brown, thick, crumbly, or separated from the nail bed.

## Possible Causes
Fungi can enter through small nail breaks and may spread from athlete foot or damp environments.

## Risk Groups
Older people and people with diabetes, circulation problems, sweaty footwear, nail injury, or reduced immunity.

## Risk Level
Usually low to moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Keep nails clean and dry and ask a clinician or pharmacist about treatment.

## Warning Signs
Seek care for painful, red, swollen, draining, rapidly changing, or persistent nail changes.
