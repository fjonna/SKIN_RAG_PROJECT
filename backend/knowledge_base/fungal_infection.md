# Fungal Infection

## Overview
Fungal skin infections can cause itchy, scaly, peeling, or ring-shaped patches.

## Skin Symptoms
Itchy rash, scaly patches, peeling skin, red border, circular rash, flaky skin, cracked skin, and irritation.

## Visual Appearance
Patches may have scaling and a more noticeable red outer edge.

## Possible Causes
Fungi can spread through contact, shared items, moisture, and warm occluded skin.

## Risk Groups
People with moist skin, shared changing-area exposure, diabetes, skin injury, or reduced immunity.

## Risk Level
Usually low to moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Keep skin clean and dry and seek advice about appropriate antifungal care.

## Warning Signs
Seek care for spreading, painful, infected, facial, scalp, or persistent rashes.
