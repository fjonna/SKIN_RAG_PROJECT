# Acne and Rosacea

## Overview
Acne causes clogged pores and inflammatory breakouts. Rosacea can cause long-term facial redness, sensitivity, and acne-like bumps.

## Skin Symptoms
Pimples, blackheads, whiteheads, papules, pustules, oily skin, flushing, redness, burning, and visible blood vessels.

## Visual Appearance
Breakouts or persistent red facial areas with visible vessels may occur.

## Possible Causes
Blocked follicles, inflammation, and individual rosacea triggers such as heat or irritating products can contribute.

## Risk Groups
Acne is common in adolescents and adults; rosacea can affect adults with easily flushed skin.

## Risk Level
Usually low to moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Use gentle skin care and seek professional advice for persistent symptoms.

## Warning Signs
Seek care for painful deep lesions, eye symptoms, scarring, or rapidly worsening redness.
