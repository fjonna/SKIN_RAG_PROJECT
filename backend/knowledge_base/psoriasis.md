# Psoriasis

## Overview
Psoriasis is an immune-mediated inflammatory condition that often produces well-defined, thick, scaly plaques and recurring flares.

## Skin Symptoms
Well-defined red plaques, silvery scales, thick scaly skin, itchy plaques, cracked plaques, scalp scaling, and inflamed patches.

## Visual Appearance
Raised red or darker plaques may have thick white or silvery scale.

## Possible Causes
Immune-system activity and triggers such as stress, infection, skin injury, or some medicines can contribute.

## Risk Groups
People of any age, especially those with a family history or certain immune and lifestyle factors.

## Risk Level
Moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Use gentle skin care and discuss recurring or extensive plaques with a clinician.

## Warning Signs
Seek care for widespread painful plaques, infection signs, major nail changes, or significant daily-life impact.
