# Psoriasis

## Overview
Psoriasis is an immune-mediated inflammatory condition that often produces well-defined, thick, scaly plaques and recurring flares.

## Skin Symptoms
Well-defined red plaques, silvery scales, thick scaly skin, itchy plaques, cracked plaques, scalp scaling, and inflamed patches.

## Visual Appearance
Raised red or darker plaques may have thick white or silvery scale.

## Distinguishing Features
The plaques are thick, raised and sharply cut off from normal skin, covered in silvery white scale that flakes off in layers; scraping it away leaves pinpoint bleeding. Distribution is strikingly symmetric, hitting both elbows or both knees together. Unlike eczema the edge is sharply defined rather than blurred and the scale is thick and silvery rather than fine. Unlike a fungal infection the plaque is uniformly involved with no clearing in the middle. Pitted nails and scaling that stops at the hairline support the diagnosis.

## Typical Location and Pattern
Outer surfaces above all -- elbows, knees, shins -- plus the scalp and its hairline, the lower back and buttock cleft, the navel, and the nails. Often improves with sunlight and worsens with stress, infection, skin injury and cold dry weather.

## Possible Causes
Immune-system activity and triggers such as stress, infection, skin injury, or some medicines can contribute.

## Risk Groups
People of any age, especially those with a family history or certain immune and lifestyle factors.

## Risk Level
Moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Use gentle skin care and discuss recurring or extensive plaques with a clinician.

## Warning Signs
Seek care for widespread painful plaques, infection signs, major nail changes, or significant daily-life impact.
