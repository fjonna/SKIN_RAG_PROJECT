# Atopic Dermatitis

## Overview
Atopic dermatitis is a common form of eczema with a sensitive, inflamed skin barrier and recurrent itchy, dry patches.

## Skin Symptoms
Intense itching, dry skin, red rash, scaly patches, cracked or thickened skin, inflammation, and weeping skin.

## Visual Appearance
Dry, red, scaly, or cracked patches may occur in recurring flares.

## Possible Causes
Skin-barrier and immune factors, irritants, allergens, climate changes, and scratching can contribute.

## Risk Groups
Children and people with a personal or family history of allergic conditions.

## Risk Level
Usually low to moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Use fragrance-free moisturizers and avoid personal irritants.

## Warning Signs
Seek care for pus, yellow crusting, painful cracking, rapid spread, or persistent flares.
