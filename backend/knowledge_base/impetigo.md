# Impetigo

## Overview
Impetigo is a contagious superficial bacterial skin infection that can form sores or blisters followed by yellow crusts.

## Skin Symptoms
Honey-colored crust, yellow crust, red sores, blisters, oozing lesions, crusted rash, and facial sores.

## Visual Appearance
Small red lesions can break and develop a golden or honey-colored crust.

## Possible Causes
Bacteria can infect broken skin and spread through contact or scratching.

## Risk Groups
Children and people with skin injury, eczema, close contact, or crowded settings.

## Risk Level
Moderate.

## Recommended Next Steps
Avoid sharing towels and seek clinical advice about treatment.

## Warning Signs
Seek prompt care for spreading lesions, painful swelling, eye involvement, or persistent symptoms.
