# Athlete Foot

## Overview
Athlete foot is a fungal infection of foot skin, often between the toes.

## Skin Symptoms
Itchy feet, peeling or flaky skin, scaling between toes, white soggy skin, cracks, red soles, and blisters.

## Visual Appearance
White, peeling, or red patches can appear between toes, on soles, or on foot sides.

## Possible Causes
Fungi thrive in warm, damp environments and can spread in shared changing areas.

## Risk Groups
People with sweaty feet, tight footwear, communal shower exposure, or damaged foot skin.

## Risk Level
Usually low.

## Recommended Next Steps
Keep feet clean and dry and seek advice about suitable antifungal care.

## Warning Signs
Seek care for spreading redness, severe pain, infection signs, or symptoms that do not improve.
