# Benign Keratosis

## Overview
Benign keratoses are noncancerous growths that can look waxy, rough, or stuck onto skin; visual appearance alone cannot confirm this.

## Skin Symptoms
Waxy growth, raised rough spot, stuck-on lesion, tan or brown patch, scaly growth, and crusty plaque.

## Visual Appearance
Often a waxy, rough, raised brown or tan growth.

## Possible Causes
These growths are common with aging and may have a genetic contribution.

## Risk Groups
Adults and people with a family history of similar growths.

## Risk Level
Usually low.

## Recommended Next Steps
Avoid picking and have uncertain lesions examined.

## Warning Signs
Seek care if a lesion changes, bleeds, becomes painful, or looks unlike other spots.
