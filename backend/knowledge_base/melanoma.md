# Melanoma

## Overview
Melanoma is a potentially serious skin cancer that can develop in an existing mole or as a new pigmented lesion.

## Skin Symptoms
Asymmetric mole, irregular border, multiple colors, dark changing spot, enlarging mole, bleeding mole, and evolving lesion.

## Visual Appearance
A mole or spot may be asymmetric, irregularly bordered, multicolored, or changing.

## Possible Causes
Ultraviolet exposure and genetic factors contribute to melanoma risk.

## Risk Groups
People with many or atypical moles, family history, intense ultraviolet exposure, or reduced immune function.

## Risk Level
High.

## Recommended Next Steps
Seek prompt dermatologist assessment for a suspicious or changing lesion.

## Warning Signs
Urgently assess a new or evolving dark lesion, especially if it bleeds or changes rapidly.
